# Acme Supply — Delivery and Freight

## Branch delivery

Orders of **$750 or more** are delivered free within 40 miles of the fulfilling branch.
Smaller orders, or orders beyond 40 miles, pay freight at the zone rates below.

## Will-call

Orders placed by **2:00 pm** are ready for same-day pickup at the branch counter.

## Freight zones

Freight is a flat **$35 handling charge** plus a per-pound rate by zone:

| Zone | Distance from branch | Rate per lb |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | up to 40 miles | $0.18 |
| B | 41 to 120 miles | $0.27 |
| C | over 120 miles | $0.41 |

Example: a 200 lb order to zone B is $35 + 200 × $0.27 = $89.00.
