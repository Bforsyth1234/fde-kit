# Acme Supply — Returns Policy

Effective January 1, 2026. Applies to all four branches.

## Return window

Contractors may return unused, unopened stock within **45 days** of the invoice date for credit
to their account. Bring the invoice number to the branch counter.

## Restocking fee

Non-defective returns carry a **15% restocking fee**, deducted from the credit. Defective items
returned under warranty carry no restocking fee.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Refrigerant cylinders (R-454B, R-32, R-410A), opened or not.
- Special-order equipment (anything ordered with an SO- number).
- Cut-to-length line sets and ductwork.
