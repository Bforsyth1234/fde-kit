# Acme Supply — Equipment Warranty

These terms cover equipment sold by Acme Supply under the Acme house brand (AcmeAir).
Manufacturer brands carry their own warranty; Acme passes it through unchanged.

| Component | Registered within 60 days of install | Not registered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compressor | 10 years, parts | 5 years, parts |
| Heat exchanger | 20 years, parts | 10 years, parts |
| All other parts | 5 years, parts | 1 year, parts |

Labor is never covered by the Acme warranty. Registration is done online by the installing
contractor using the unit serial number.

A warranty claim requires the failed part, the unit serial number and the install date.
