# Acme Supply — Contractor Pricing Tiers

Tiers are set every January from the contractor's spend in the previous calendar year.

| Tier | Prior-year spend | Discount off list | Payment terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | under $50,000 | 0% | due on receipt |
| Silver | $50,000 to $149,999 | 4% | net 30 |
| Gold | $150,000 or more | 8% | net 30 |

Overdue balances on net-30 accounts accrue a late charge of 1.5% per month.
